## The rules for these six weeks

*State them every cycle. They are the half of the bargain that people forget by week three.*

- **Uninterrupted time.** *Teams get the full cycle. Say who is absorbing interruptions instead, by name or by team. If the answer is nobody, the cycle will absorb them and you should say that too.*
- **Fixed time, variable scope.** *The date does not move. Scope is what gives.*
- **The circuit breaker.** *Unfinished work does not get an automatic extension. It goes back to shaping and can be re-bet later.*
- **Who to ask.** *Who decides when a team hits something the pitch did not cover.*

## Notes on using this template

*Delete this section too.*

**This is the only announcement.** There is no separate planning meeting, no task breakdown session, no estimation. The team opens the pitch and starts. If your cycle needs a kick-off meeting to make sense, the pitch was not shaped well enough and that is the thing to fix.

**Name the people, not the roles.** "Design and backend" is a staffing plan. Two names is a team, and a team is what the method assumes.

**Do not restate the pitch.** The pitch is frozen and linked. Any summary you write here becomes a second source that says something slightly different by week two.

**Post the cool-down dates now.** People plan around cool-down, and publishing the dates at kick-off is what stops the next cycle quietly starting early.

**Where this lives:** wherever the team already talks. It is an announcement with a short life. It needs to be read on the day, not found in two years.
