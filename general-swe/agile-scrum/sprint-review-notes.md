## Notes on using this template

*Delete this section too.*

**There is no sign-off, and adding one costs you something real.** Scrum has no Product Owner acceptance step at the review; acceptance is the Definition of Done, applied continuously through the Sprint. A review functioning as an approval gate has reintroduced a stage gate, and the Guide addresses this directly: the Sprint Review "should never be considered a gate to releasing value". If your notes have an approval column, that is where to look first.

**Absent stakeholders are the finding.** The most common failure of this event is not a bad demonstration; it is an empty room. Record who was invited and did not attend, Sprint after Sprint. That pattern is a conversation the Product Owner needs to have, and it is invisible in any single set of notes.

**Timebox: four hours for a one-month Sprint**, proportionally less for shorter ones.

**Write it during the session, not after.** Reconstructed reactions become paraphrases of what the team already believed.

**Where this lives:** the wiki, linked from the Sprint in the tracker. The audience is wider than the team and includes people who will not open a repository.
